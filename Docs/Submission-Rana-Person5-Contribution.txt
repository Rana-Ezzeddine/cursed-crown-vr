Identity and scope
Rana Ezzeddine — Person 5, CMPS297AG, The Cursed Crown VR. Prepared 14 November 2026. This statement describes my own work and the material I reused. It does not make declarations on behalf of Carla, Rochan, Jad or Elio.

My assigned contribution
My assigned system was the crown objective and match lifecycle: validated pickup, single-carrier ownership, crown release on death/disconnect, possession-based scoring, readiness, host-only start/restart, the synchronized round deadline, final scores and shared wins on ties. The main implementation is CrownMatchRules, MatchClock, MatchController, MatchPresentation and Person5HealthBridge.
I worked on integrating these features with the team’s session, avatar, arena, spawn/drop, weapon and health systems. My role included defining and refining requirements, reviewing outcomes, providing headset feedback, requesting and checking fixes, and preparing the demonstration/submission. The implementation was substantially AI-assisted, not unaided.

Later integration and presentation work
I directed and tested later refinements on integration/final-game: clearer controller-operated menus, hiding lobby controls during a round, health/crown/time/charge feedback, reachable crown approaches and more teleport guidance, temple presentation and crown visuals, five cosmetic character choices, lobby name entry, networked names and the Hall of Champions leaderboard. I requested teleport-only movement after trying continuous walking, and later requested reducing shot damage to 10 per hit.
These additions extend my teammates’ work. They do not make me the original author of the session foundation, arena/spawn management, weapons or health/respawn. Character bodies are procedural and stylized; only the head and hands are tracked, with approximated limbs.
